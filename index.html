14-Day Hassle-Free Returns & Exchanges

Under Egyptian Law 181 of 2018, you may return or exchange any garment within 14 calendar days of delivery for any reason (sizing, fit, or preference). The item must be in its original unworn condition, unwashed, with all TEMPORARY™ labels, barcode tags, and physical wax seal attached.
