05 / RETURNS

Return &
exchange.

Egypt consumer-return information is presented clearly below, with the legal exceptions kept visible.

14-day return / exchange

For eligible goods, the Egyptian Consumer Protection Agency states that the consumer may exchange or return a product and recover its cash value within 14 days of receipt without giving a reason and without additional expenses, subject to the stated statutory exceptions.

Important for TEMPORARY™: a product made to customer-specified special specifications is one of the listed exceptions when it conforms to those specifications. Customized garments may therefore fall within this exception.
  • The goods must not have been altered or made impossible to return to their original condition because of the consumer.
  • Certain goods are excluded by nature, packaging, perishability or other statutory exceptions.

30-day defective-product right

For a product with a defect, the Egyptian Consumer Protection Agency states that the consumer may request replacement or return with a cash refund within 30 days of receipt. The supplier is required, upon request, to replace or refund without additional cost within one week, with the refund made using the same payment method.

If there is a dispute about whether a defect exists, the Consumer Protection Agency can examine the complaint and issue a binding decision under the applicable law.

This page is informational and based on the Egyptian Consumer Protection Agency's published return/exchange guidance and Consumer Protection Law No. 181 of 2018. Product-specific rights may depend on the facts and statutory exceptions.
